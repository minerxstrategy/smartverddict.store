What is ProDentim?

ProDentim is a dietary supplement presented in a convenient chewable tablet format, specifically engineered to balance the oral microbiome. It was developed for adults seeking a natural way to complement their daily dental hygiene routine by introducing a blend of 3.5 billion colony-forming units (CFU) of probiotic strains and essential nutrients.

What sets this product apart is its shift away from standard oral care products that often contain harsh chemicals. While traditional mouthwashes and toothpastes can inadvertently eliminate both harmful and beneficial bacteria, this supplement focuses on repopulation, treating the root of oral imbalance rather than just addressing symptoms.

The product works by introducing beneficial bacteria directly into the oral cavity to outcompete harmful microbes. Upon chewing the tablet, the probiotics begin to colonize the mouth, helping to support gum health, maintain tooth strength, and promote a healthier respiratory system.
